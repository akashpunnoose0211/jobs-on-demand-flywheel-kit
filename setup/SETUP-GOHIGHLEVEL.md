# Set up GoHighLevel (for beginners)
**Jobs-On-Demand Flywheel Kit · read this first if you don't have GoHighLevel yet**

> GoHighLevel is where your five agents live and run every day: your contacts, texts, calendar, automations, chat bot and team alerts, all in one account. This guide covers phase 1 of the install guide (`INSTALL-GUIDE.md` (in the main kit folder)) for GoHighLevel, in about an hour of your time. The texting approval in step 5 then takes a few days.

## What you need before you start
- Your business's legal name, address and tax ID (EIN), exactly as the IRS has them. The texting approval checks these.
- A website with a privacy policy page. The texting approval usually requires one.
- A credit card for GoHighLevel and for texts and calls.
- Admin access to your Facebook business page and your Google Business listing.

## Two words you'll see everywhere
- **Agency account:** the top level GoHighLevel gives you when you sign up. Think of it as the building.
- **Sub-account:** one business inside it. Think of it as your office. **Everything in this kit happens inside your sub-account.** Most owners only ever need one.

## Step by step

1. `You` **Sign up.** Go to gohighlevel.com and start the free trial. Choose a plan that includes workflows (GoHighLevel's name for automations) and Conversation AI (its name for the chat bot). On some plans the chat bot is a paid add-on. Prices change, so check the pricing page.
   Done when: you can log in.

2. `You` **Create your business's sub-account.** In the agency view, open **Sub-Accounts** and create one with your business name, address, phone, website and time zone. Then switch into it (the account switcher sits at the top left).
   Done when: the top left shows your business name, not the agency's.

3. `You` **Fill in your business profile.** In your sub-account, go to **Settings**, then **Business Profile**. Check the name, address, time zone, logo and website.
   Done when: every field is filled and the time zone is yours.

4. `You` **Add a card for texts and calls.** Texts, calls and AI replies are billed by use from a wallet. Add your card, and turn on auto-recharge so texts never stop. Set a low recharge amount at first.
   Done when: the wallet shows a balance and auto-recharge is on.

5. `You` **Get your phone number and start the texting approval.** In **Settings**, then **Phone Numbers**, buy a local number (or follow the steps to bring your main number over). Then fill in the business texting registration (GoHighLevel calls it A2P 10DLC, in its Trust Center). Use the exact legal name, address and tax ID. `SETUP-TEXTING-APPROVAL.md` (in this folder) walks you through every box, with words you can paste. **Do this today.** Approval often takes several days, and no automatic text goes out until it's approved.
   Done when: the registration shows as submitted.

6. `You` **Add your team.** In **Settings**, then **My Staff** (or **Team**), add each person who will get alerts or work leads. Give each their own login, never a shared one.
   Done when: every person on your alert lists has a user.

7. `You` **Put the GoHighLevel app on every team phone.** Each person downloads the **LeadConnector** app from their app store, logs in with their own user, and turns notifications on. This is how the agents' alerts reach their phones.
   Done when: every person has logged in on their phone, with notifications allowed.

8. `You` **Connect Facebook and Instagram.** In **Settings**, then **Integrations**, connect your Facebook page (and Instagram). This lets the chat bot answer messages and brings Facebook form leads in.
   Done when: a message to your page from your personal Facebook shows up in **Conversations**.

9. `You` **Connect Google.** In the same place, connect your Google Business listing. Then connect your sales rep's Google calendar under **Calendars** so bookings land on it.
   Done when: your Google listing shows as connected, and a test booking appears on the rep's calendar.

10. `You` **Make the key your AI will use.** In **Settings**, then **Private Integrations**, create a new one called "Flywheel kit AI". Give it access to contacts, conversations, custom fields, custom values, tags, opportunities, calendars, users and workflows. Copy the key it shows, and give it straight to Claude Code when it asks on day 1. Don't email it or paste it in a document.
    Done when: the key exists. You'll hand it over on day 1.

11. `You` **Import the kit's template, if you were given one.** If your thank-you page includes a GoHighLevel snapshot link (a one-click template), open it, pick your sub-account and import. Everything arrives switched off. If you didn't get one, skip this. Claude Code builds everything with you instead.
    Done when: imported, or skipped.

## Things that trip up beginners
- **Working in the agency view by mistake.** If you can't find Conversations or Calendars, you're in the agency view. Switch into your sub-account.
- **The texting approval comes back rejected.** Usually the business name, address or tax ID doesn't match the IRS records, or the website has no privacy policy. Fix it and resubmit. Facebook messages still work while you wait.
- **Menus moved.** GoHighLevel changes its menus often. If a name here isn't where this guide says, use the search bar inside GoHighLevel.
- **Alerts don't reach a phone.** Check that the person is logged into the LeadConnector app with their own user and that the phone allows its notifications.

Done when: steps 1 to 10 are done (11 if you have a template). Then open `INSTALL-GUIDE.md` (in the main kit folder) and go to phase 2.
