# Team card

**Jobs-On-Demand Flywheel Kit · for the sales rep, the office, the customer experience owner and the crew**

> Five agents now answer, follow up, check in, make ads and keep the numbers. They never close a job. You do. This card says what to do when an alert lands, what to mark, and how to take over a lead.

## Start here

1. **Log into the LeadConnector app** (GoHighLevel's phone app) and turn notifications on. Your alerts land there, unless your owner picked a text or chat app.
2. **When an alert lands, do the one thing it says,** from the business line. Then mark the task.
3. **Mark every visit the same day:** held, no-show, cancelled or moved.
4. **Fill in the quote fields** the moment a quote goes out, and again when the homeowner decides.
5. **If a lead needs a person, run the takeover play below:** call first, then text, then log it.

Alerts go only to the team, never to a customer. Outside your alert hours, an alert waits and lands first thing when they start. If your business uses the fallback, each alert shows up as a task instead of a phone notice.

## The takeover play

This is the kit's one takeover play, for every agent. Save the lead first and find the cause later. Never fix the setup while a customer waits.

1. **Pause the bot.** Add the tag `stop bot` (a label on the contact that stops every automatic message). Add a note: "Takeover started", with the date and time.
2. **Read before you reach out.** Read the whole conversation and the contact's fields. Never ask twice for something they gave.
3. **Call first, then text.** Call from the business line. No answer, or no phone yet? Send the matching text below.
4. **Collect only what is missing:** name, phone, zip, job type, a short note and how urgent. No phone after one more ask? Move them to Needs Review and stop asking.
5. **Hand over, or close.** A fit goes on the Lead Transfer form. Not interested goes to Not Interested. A bad fit gets a polite close, the tag `ai dq`, and Out of Area or Out of Scope.
6. **Log one row in the Failure Log** (a tab in the scoreboard spreadsheet). Write the exact failure, never just "the bot failed."

The two takeover texts, typed by a person:

> **Details still missing:** "Hey [FIRST NAME], jumping in here so we can make sure this gets handled correctly. Can you send me the best phone number and zip code for the property?"

> **Details already given:** "Got it, thanks. I have [JOB TYPE] in [CITY OR ZIP]. I'm getting this to the right person so they can help with the next step."

Fill in every line of the Failure Log row:

```
FAILURE LOG ROW
Date and time:
Lead:
Who took over:
Lead source:
What the agent did:
What the customer said:
Missing or wrong field:
What you did:
Lead saved? yes / no
Fix needed:
```

The same failure three times means fix the setup, not the lead.

## What to mark, and when

| When this happens | Mark this | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| You call a new lead | The call task: "called" or "no answer" | The caller | Right after the call |
| A visit gets booked | Tag `booked` and Visit Date | The calendar does it. If not, the person who books. | When it is booked |
| The visit day ends | **Visit Outcome:** held, no-show, cancelled or moved. If moved, put the new time in Visit Date. | The sales rep on that visit | The same day |
| A quote goes out | **Quote Sent At** and **Quote Amount** | The sales rep | When the quote goes out |
| An open quote | One owner and a Next Follow-up date | The quote owner | Every open quote, always |
| The homeowner decides | **Decision Status:** won or lost. Won: **Signed Date** and **Signed Amount**. Lost: one **Lost Reason** plus a short note and the date. | The quote owner | The day they decide |
| The job is finished | **Job Completed At** and **Completion Owner**, at the Work Complete stage | The person who answers for the job | When the job is finished |
| A check-in reply is unclear or mixed | **Quality Status:** happy, unresolved, unhappy or unreachable | A person, never a rule | When it comes in |
| A review shows up | **Review Completed:** yes | A person | When you see it |
| A new caller you don't know | First source, picked from the list | Whoever answers | On the first call |

Why it matters: "no-show" starts the rescue texts, "moved" restarts the reminders, and Quote Sent At starts the quote texts. Job Completed At starts the next-day check-in. The Friday numbers count only what you marked.

**Lost reasons. Pick one, and never write only "bad lead".**

| | |
|---|---|
| Price or budget | Scope or service mismatch |
| Financing unavailable or declined | Trust or proof concern |
| Timing or not ready | Could not reach after the full follow-up |
| Chose a competitor | Solved it another way |
| Decision-makers not agreed | Duplicate or invalid |

## One lead, start to finish (a made-up example)

Oak Street Concrete lifts sunken slabs. Any trade works the same way. Maria is the sales rep. The business line is (555) 010-2000. Pat Sample, (555) 010-4477, fills in a form on a Facebook ad on Monday at 9:12 AM.

**Monday, 9:12 AM. The form lead lands.**

Pat gets, at once:

> Hey Pat, it's Maria with Oak Street Concrete. Got your request about the free estimate. I'm going to call you in the next couple minutes from (555) 010-2000 so it's not a random number. If now's bad, just text me a better time.

The team gets:

> New form lead:\
> Pat Sample,\
> Driveway lifting,\
> 37209.\
> Call now: (555) 010-4477.

**9:14 AM. The call.** Maria calls from the business line. Pat picks up. Maria marks the task "called", so no "Still not called" alert goes out. Had Pat not answered, Maria would mark "no answer", and Pat would get a "Just tried you!" text 5 minutes later.

**9:20 AM. The booking.** Maria books a free estimate for Wednesday at 4:00 PM. The calendar adds the tag `booked` and fills Visit Date. The lead follow-up stops and the reminders start.

**9:25 AM. The reminders start.** Pat gets:

> Hi Pat, it's Oak Street Concrete. You're booked for your free estimate on Wednesday at 4:00 PM. Does that time still work?

Pat replies "yes". A plain yes keeps the reminders going, and no alert goes out. Any other reply would stop them and send the team "Booked homeowner replied".

Pat also gets, on schedule:

> **2 days before:** "Before your free estimate, here's a recent job we finished nearby. We'll look at the sunken driveway slab and walk you through the next step." (with one before-and-after photo)

> **Tuesday:** "Quick reminder: your free estimate is tomorrow at 4:00 PM. If anything changed, just reply here."

> **Wednesday morning:** "Your free estimate is today at 4:00 PM. If anything changed, reply here and we'll find a new time."

> **Wednesday, 3:00 PM:** "See you in about an hour. Keep an eye out for (555) 010-2000."

**Wednesday, 4:00 PM. The visit.** Maria visits and sets Visit Outcome to held the same day. That ends the reminders. If she forgot, she would get this at the evening check time:

> No outcome yet:\
> Pat Sample,\
> Free estimate today at 4:00 PM.\
> Mark it held, no-show, cancelled or moved.

**Wednesday, 5:15 PM. The quote.** Maria sends the proposal and fills Quote Sent At and Quote Amount. The quote texts start. Pat gets, right away:

> Just sent the proposal for driveway lifting. Can you give me a quick thumbs-up when it lands? That way I know the photos and options came through.

Pat replies "Got it, thanks!" Any reply stops the automatic quote texts. The team gets:

> Quote reply:\
> Pat Sample,\
> Driveway lifting.\
> They said: 'Got it, thanks!'.\
> Reply to them now, from the business number.

Maria replies by hand and follows up. The day Pat decides, the quote owner sets Decision Status. If Pat signs, they also fill Signed Date and Signed Amount. When the crew finishes the job, Job Completed At starts the reviews agent's check-in the next business day.

## Good to know

- **Call before you text.** A takeover text goes out only after a call gets no answer, or when there is no phone yet.
- **Calls and texts come from the business line.** Never from your own cell.
- **One alert, one action.** Do the one thing it says, then close the task.
- **Never ask for a five-star review.** Ask for an honest one. Never offer anything for it.
- **Alerts stopped landing?** Check the LeadConnector app, then work the call task list by hand.
