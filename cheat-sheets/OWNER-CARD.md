# Owner card

**Jobs-On-Demand Flywheel Kit · for the owner · one page**

> Open your kit folder in Claude Code and type one of the lines below. Claude Code reads the kit's files and leads from there.

## What to type to Claude Code

| Type this | What it does |
|---|---|
| **start the flywheel kit** | Day 1. Sets up your software, starts the texting approval, fills in `MY-NUMBERS.md` with you and makes your test contact. |
| **what's next?** | Reads `PROGRESS.md`. Tells you which day you are on, what is done, and the one next step. |
| **install my reviews agent** | Day 2. Builds the check-in, review ask, referral offer and alerts A to H. |
| **install my booking agent, the Facebook message part** | Day 3. Builds the chat bot, the hand-off and the "needs a person" alert. |
| **finish my booking agent: form leads and calls** | Day 4. Adds the instant text, the call tasks and the "Still not called" alert. |
| **install my follow-up agent** | Day 5. Builds the quiet-lead texts, the visit reminders and the quote follow-up. |
| **install my data agent, part 1** | Day 6. Builds the scoreboard spreadsheet, the evening outcome reminder and the Friday review. |
| **install my ads agent** | Day 7. Builds the crew alerts, the starting nine ads and the spend check, all switched off. |
| **install my [booking / follow-up / reviews / ads / data] agent** | Installs that one agent by itself, from its file. |
| **install my data agent, part 2** | Optional, after a few Fridays. Sets up your own database so Friday's numbers fill with nobody typing. |
| **check my first week** | Each day of your first week live. Checks spend, leads, bot replies, hand-offs and call speed. After 7 days, it writes the fixes into `PROGRESS.md`. |
| **I'm stuck on day [number], step [number]. Here is what I see.** | Add a screenshot, or describe the screen. Claude Code helps you past it. |

Every agent is built switched off, with a filter that lets only your test contact through.

## Your rhythm

| When | Time | What to look at |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | A few minutes | The Needs Review list. Quotes with no owner, or a follow-up past due. Every finished job has a quality status and one owner. Unhappy jobs have their asks paused. |
| **Weekly** | About an hour, on ads | Post 2 to 3 Reels (short videos). Read chats, form answers and call notes for the words homeowners use. Read the week's spend changes in the log. Keep a phone note of every homeowner question. |
| **Monday** | A glance | The Monday numbers message: last week's counts next to the week before. |
| **Friday** | 20 minutes, at your review time | Your office exports the week's contacts. Claude fills the scoreboard. Read the numbers alerts first. Then decide keep, fix, rotate or pause on each live ad. Every call goes in the Decision Log. |

The number to watch most is **cost per booked appointment**: ad spend divided by booked visits. Never judge an ad on cost per lead or clicks.

## Your yes is needed for

- **New or changed wording,** before any customer sees it.
- **Each new ad.** You get "New ad to approve". Reply YES to launch. No answer keeps it off.
- **Each go-live.** An agent turns on only after every test passes and you say yes.
- **Ad spend start.** The spend check runs in practice mode, saying only what it would change, until the approver says start.
- **Anything new in ads:** a new audience, area, offer, service or campaign.

Reply **UNDO** to any "Ad spend change" alert to reverse it. If you named someone else as the approver, these yeses are theirs.
