# Install your ads agent
**Jobs-On-Demand Flywheel Kit · Brand · agent 4 of 5**

> This agent turns the jobs your crews already do into new ads and posts every month.

## What this agent does
- Sends push alerts to tell the crew what to film, edits every video, writes every ad, launches every ad, and manages ad spend automatically.
- Crew alerts on each job day. One filming visit a month stays as the backup.
- Starts with about nine ads in three angles, then adds three to five new ads a month from the angle that books.
- Every ad opens on the homeowner's problem, never on your company.
- Each new ad launches only on [APPROVER NAME]'s yes.
- Spend moves by written rules, never above [MONTHLY AD BUDGET]. Every change is logged and sent to [APPROVER NAME]. Payment and ad account ownership stay with a person.

## What you need before you start
- A smartphone and a tripod.
- A Facebook page for the business, or the facts to create one (name, category, photos).
- Access to the right Meta Business Manager and ad account, with payment active. (Meta owns Facebook and Instagram.)
- A customer list tool (CRM) that records each new lead and which ad it came from.
- Your booking agent, or a person, ready to reply first to a lead from a Messenger chat or an instant form. For ads to a call, a person who answers the business line during [PHONE HOURS].
- Ads go to three places: a Messenger chat, a short instant form, or a phone call. The first nine go to the instant form. Messenger and call ads come later (step 11).
- At least one Google review you can screenshot, and real job photos for the first nine ads.
- One real job coming up that you can film before, during and after.
- Your facts: company name, trade, [CITY], [SERVICE AREAS], [RADIUS] (miles you travel), and your offer (for example: a free estimate). Each place uses Facebook's own button: Send Message for Messenger ads, Get Quote for form ads, Call Now for call ads.
- From MY-NUMBERS.md, "Alerts and ad spend": [ALERT CHANNEL], [CREW ALERT LIST], [ALERT HOURS], [MONTHLY AD BUDGET], [APPROVER NAME], [COST PER APPOINTMENT LINE], [MONEY PER AD A DAY], [DAYS BETWEEN RAISES], [CLIPS FOLDER] and [EDITOR NAME]. You write each one; the agent never picks them.
- Seven short lines, if you have them. If you do not, the AI drafts them in step 4 from your problem bank and you approve them:
  - Opening line: one yes-or-no question about the problem.
  - Homeowner line: one or two sentences on the problem as a local homeowner feels it.
  - Climate line: the local condition, such as weather or ground, that causes the problem.
  - Seasonal triggers: the times of year or events that make it worse.
  - Authority line: what you specialize in. No made-up credentials.
  - Designed-for line: why you are a fit for this market.
  - Service areas line: your list of towns.

**What your tools must do.** Your AI assistant checks these before step 1. Without the tool, the way around runs, by hand.
- **Crew alerts:** your job schedule or customer tool runs a morning automation for the day's jobs and sends alerts through [ALERT CHANNEL]. Way around: a task for the crew lead and a daily job list.
- **Editing:** a video editor your AI assistant can run, or one that edits from written instructions. Way around: a cut sheet [EDITOR NAME] follows.
- **Launching and spend:** a way for your AI assistant to work in your ad account: build ads, turn them on and off, change budgets. Way around: a person clicks as the AI directs.
- **Running by itself:** an AI assistant that runs on a schedule and sends alerts. Way around: a person opens it each morning.
- **Booked appointments by ad:** the Scoreboard rows from your data agent (file 05), or counts your office reads out weekly. No count, no spend change.


## Step by step

1. `You` **Pick one starting point.** Choose one area, one problem, one service, one offer and one next step. Example: "Homeowners near [CITY] with [VISIBLE PROBLEM] after [TRIGGER]."
   Done when: you can say the target in one sentence, with a named market and problem.

2. `You + AI` **Build the problem bank and fear map.** The AI lists the signs a homeowner sees for your trade (for example: water on the floor after heavy rain). For each sign it writes what they fear, what ignoring it costs, why they put it off, and what they must believe to act. It also lists the false fixes they try first and their top questions: is it expensive, can it wait, do you serve my area. You confirm it matches what local homeowners say.
   Done when: every sign has a fear map, and the false-fix and question lists exist.

3. `AI` **Save a reference file.** Save 5 or more ad structures from contractors in other markets and from the Meta Ad Library. Borrow the format or the order. Rewrite every word.
   Done when: 5 or more are saved and none is copied word for word.

4. `You + AI` **Write the ad words for the starting nine.** The angles and formats are under TESTING in the instructions. The AI builds the words from your seven lines or a base ad below, swapping in your company, market, radius, service areas and offer. If your first visit is not free, it takes out the word "free". [VISIT TYPE] is what you call the first visit (for example: inspection, estimate). One service per ad. You approve the final words.
   Done when: each of the nine meets RULES FOR WORDS, and its service areas match the targeting.

5. `You + AI` **Write the shot lists.** The job list rides on every crew alert: the four shots (before, doing, after, proof) plus one extra shot the AI picks each month from that month's ad ideas. The monthly list is for the backup filming visit: 3 to 5 new ad ideas, 3 to 4 posts and the five trust videos. Every batch covers three buckets: proof (the work and the result), teach (a sign, process, tool or false fix explained) and entertain (satisfying field footage). Skip a post that fits none. You approve both lists.
   Done when: both lists are approved and cover the three buckets and the four shots.

6. `You + AI` **Set up crew alerts.** You share [CLIPS FOLDER] with [CREW ALERT LIST]. The AI builds alerts 1 and 2 (CREW ALERTS in the instructions), or the way around. Film a customer only with their yes; how your team asks is your call.
   Done when: the crew alert test passes and every crew member can save a clip to the folder.

7. `You` **Keep the monthly filming visit as the backup,** for months with too few filmed jobs. One afternoon, 2 to 3 hours. Put the phone in one spot and mark the ground. Film a slow vertical pan before the work. Return to the same mark after. For talking videos: phone at eye level, look just past the lens, one take, keep rolling through stumbles. Film the five trust videos too.
   Done when: you know when the backup visit would run, and who films it.

8. `You + AI` **Set up the page and the tracking.** You click; the AI checks.
   - Page: the name matches your website and Google profile. Category is Contractor. Photo, cover and About are complete. A new page has 3 to 5 real posts live.
   - Business: correct Business Manager and ad account, payment active, no stray admins.
   - Tracking: save the Page ID. Each place the ads send people must carry the ad's name into your CRM:
     - Messenger chat: create an events dataset, the record that ties messages back to ads, and save its Dataset ID. In Events Manager: Data Sources, Connect Data Source, Messaging, Facebook Page. Pick your page, then connect the dataset to the ad account.
     - Instant form: pass the ad name with the form, and connect the form to your CRM so each lead lands there at once, where the booking agent's form lead fast reply picks it up. If your CRM cannot receive form leads by itself, tell your AI assistant before you go on.
     - Phone call: the ad rings the forwarding number for Meta ads from the data agent (file 05, its trackable path step), which rings your business line.
   - One path per ad: ad, then the chat, the form or the call, then the lead saved in your CRM with its ad.
   Done when: every box is checked, the Page ID (and the Dataset ID, for Messenger) is saved, and each place you will run passes its test lead.

9. `You + AI` **Edit the videos.** The AI edits by the EDITING lines in the instructions, or writes a cut sheet and sends alert 3. A cut sheet lists, in order: each clip's file name, its start and end, the words on screen, the captions, and the file name to save as.
   Done when: one real job's clips became a video that passes the video test, by tool or by cut sheet.

10. `You` **Give the AI access to the ad account.** The account's owner gives the AI the lowest access that builds ads, turns them on and off and changes budgets, without payment or admin rights if the account allows. No way in? Skip this; a person clicks as the AI directs, from here on.
    Done when: the AI can build an ad switched off and has no payment or admin rights, or PROGRESS.md says the account cannot leave them out.

11. `You + AI` **Build the campaign, switched off.** The AI builds it with access. Without access, it directs and you click.
    - One market, one service, one path. One campaign with one ad set, holding the starting nine.
    - Objective: Leads. Set to conversations for a Messenger chat (if Meta does not offer it, use Engagement set to conversations, and check each week until it appears), to an instant form for a form, or to calls for a call, as Meta offers them.
    - The starting nine go to a short instant form. Do not change the path while you are testing ads. Messenger and call ads are added once the first nine have booked visits, each new place on [APPROVER NAME]'s yes. For call ads, set up the missed-call alert in the booking agent (agent 1, its push alerts step).
    - Daily budget on the campaign, not the ad set, so money flows to the ads doing best. Start it at the number of ads times [MONEY PER AD A DAY]. If that would pass [MONTHLY AD BUDGET] over the month, cut no ads: send alert 6. The owner raises the budget or runs longer.
    - Build the area by hand. Never carry over cities, states, datasets or pictures from an old campaign.
    - Audience: ages 35 to 65+, never below 30, all genders, broad. Check placements by hand. Assign the correct dataset.
    - Turn OFF: Advantage+ creative, AI text, headline generation, Related Media, Multi-advertiser Ads, text improvements and Meta recommendations.
    - Paste the approved words. Use real photos or job video from the same service.
    - Never aim at raw leads or clicks.
    - Instant form: one buying question, full name, phone, email, ZIP code. Ask for the street address only when lead quality, not volume, is your problem. Sorting questions that close the form on a "no": Do you own the home? Are you looking to fix this within the next three months? Leave the budget question off at launch. Only people who pass count as leads.
    - Leave the campaign switched off.
    Done when: the campaign exists, is off, holds the nine, and passes the words check in the test list.

12. `You + AI` **Check the path from each ad.** Tap an ad preview. Messenger chat: the first reply starts fast and asks one easy thing; zip code is cleanest, since it confirms the service area first. Instant form: it asks only the fields above and closes on a "no". Phone call: it rings your business line. Add no giveaway or extra automation in front of any of them.
    Done when: the path check in the test list passes.

13. `You + AI` **Run one test lead, then launch on a yes.** Use an ad preview as your own test contact: message it, fill in its form, or call it. When all six test lead checks pass, the AI sends alert 4 for each ad. On [APPROVER NAME]'s yes, it turns the campaign on, or tells you which switch to click. Later ads follow LAUNCHING in the instructions.
    Done when: all six checks pass, [APPROVER NAME] said yes, and the campaign is on.

14. `You + AI` **Set up the spend rules.** If your ad account offers a spending limit, the person who handles payment sets one as a backstop each month. The AI sets the morning spend check and logs each change in the data agent's Decision Log (file 05), or its own log. It starts in practice mode: alert 5 for each change it would make, and no change.
    Done when: the spend tests pass and [APPROVER NAME] says the practice alerts are right and it may start.

15. `You + AI` **Keep the weekly rhythm.** About an hour a week. Post 2 to 3 Reels (short videos). Read the chats, form answers and call notes for new words homeowners use. Read the week's spend changes in the log. Keep a phone note of every homeowner question. That is your content calendar.
    Done when: the weekly hour is on the calendar.

```
RULES FOR WORDS
1. Open on the problem the homeowner can see. Never on the company, its founding date or its awards.
2. Write for the homeowner who has the problem and has not started shopping.
3. One service per ad. Never mix services.
4. Every ad names the service, the visible problem, the local market, the service area, the offer and the exact next step.
5. Service areas in the ad match the targeting. The market name is the same top and bottom.
6. One main text and one headline per ad. No pools of variants.
7. Borrow structure from the reference file, never the words.
8. The words on a picture name what the picture shows.
9. Leave no blank unfilled.

RULES FOR VIDEO
1. Four beats: opening 0-2 seconds, teach 2-20, reframe 20-28, soft ask 28-30.
2. Show the problem in the first 2 seconds.
3. Real job, real house, real crew. No stock photos, no made-up people.
4. Captions on every video.
5. One plain next step: say the button out loud, "Tap Get Quote below." (Send Message on a Messenger ad, Call Now on a call ad.)

CREW ALERTS
Each morning, alert 1 for every job on the day's schedule. The day
after, alert 2 if that job has no clips in [CLIPS FOLDER].
Never film a customer, their house number or their kids without the
customer's yes.

EDITING
From the clips: order the shots into the four beats, trim, put the
opening line on screen, burn in captions, keep before and after pairs
together, export vertical. Name the file the way the ad will be named.
No editing tool: write a cut sheet and send alert 3.

EVERY MONTH
Write the monthly shot list: 3 to 5 new ad ideas, 3 to 4 posts, the 5 trust videos, the four shots.
Cover three buckets: proof, teach, entertain. Cut any post that fits none.
Update the extra shot on alert 1.
Count new ads this month. If too few jobs were filmed, tell [APPROVER NAME] it is time for the backup filming visit.

LAUNCHING
Build each new ad switched off, in the approved campaign, beside the old
ads. Send alert 4. Turn it on only after [APPROVER NAME]'s yes.
Never edit a live ad set. Duplicate it and change the copy, as a new
ad through alert 4.

TESTING
Start with about nine ads in one ad set: three angles in three formats.
Angles: owner intro with your offer; teaching (one warning sign or fact
a homeowner did not know); problem (a pain homeowners already have with
contractors in your trade).
Formats: photo with a headline, swipe-through photos, 15-second clip
with a voiceover. Real job photos only.
Week one tells you which angle to film. It never picks a winner.
Weeks two and three, film that angle in this order: the owner, in a
place locals know, naming the town and the problem; a team member who
does the work; a real, named customer, with their yes.
Each month add 3 to 5 new ads beside the old, mostly from the angle
that books. Change one thing per new ad: format, offer or angle.
Never run only one or two ads and call the channel a failure.
One to three ads taking most of the spend is normal.
Judge only on booked appointments and their cost, never cost per lead,
clicks or views. Aim for at least three live ads that have each booked.
When an ad wears out or is turned off, draft the next from a real
homeowner question, the winning angle or a base ad.

AD SPEND RULES (each morning)
Read each ad's Spend, Booked and Cost per booked appointment from the
data agent's Scoreboard rows for each ad (file 05), or the office's
counts. Add up the last three weekly rows for a 21-day view.
The line is [COST PER APPOINTMENT LINE].
1. First 7 days of an ad: do not judge it. Only check for no spend,
   rejected or wrong link. If so, send alert 6.
2. After 21 days, put each ad in one pile:
   KEEP: 3 or more booked appointments, each at or under the line.
   OFF: spent 3 times the line or more, booked nothing. Turn it off.
   NOT TESTED YET: spent less than the line. Give it a second round.
3. The budget sits on the campaign, so an ad turned off sends its money
   to the ads still on.
4. Campaign cost per booked appointment over the last 21 days at or
   under the line: raise the budget about 20%, at most once every
   [DAYS BETWEEN RAISES] days. Never double it.
5. Over the line: cut about 20%, wait two days, look again before
   changing any ad.
6. Daily budget per live ad under [MONEY PER AD A DAY]: do not cut ads
   to fit. Send alert 6.
7. Slow month: never turn every ad off. Send alert 6.
8. Never rebuild after one bad week. Never widen the area because calls
   dip. Turn ads off; never delete one that has spent.
Change nothing and send alert 6 when: an ad has no Booked count, fits
no pile, or these rules and the data agent's Friday review disagree.
Log every change (date, what, before, after, rule, numbers) and send
alert 5. On UNDO from [APPROVER NAME], reverse it and log that.

HARD STOPS (whatever a rule says)
- Month's spend never passes [MONTHLY AD BUDGET]. Before a raise, add
  this month's spend to the new daily budget times the days left. Too
  high: no raise. Each morning, if real spend is on track to pass,
  lower the daily budget and send alert 5.
- No new audience, area, offer, service or campaign without
  [APPROVER NAME]'s yes.
- Never change a live ad's words or picture.
- Never turn on an unapproved ad.
- Never open or change payment, billing, spending limits, ad account
  ownership or admin rights.
```

## When a person takes over
- Every new ad and post waits for [APPROVER NAME]'s yes. No answer means it stays off.
- Any test fails. Ads stay off and spend rules stay in practice mode until a person fixes it.
- A fact is missing or unclear. The agent asks. It does not guess.
- No real photo or video from the same service. The ad is not ready.
- Meta does not offer the place you picked (conversations, an instant form or calls). A person checks weekly.
- A new place for the ads. [APPROVER NAME] decides.
- After week one, a person picks the angle to film. Each month, a person decides what to test next and who goes on camera. The agent counts new ads and reminds.
- A tool is missing. Its way around, under "What you need", runs instead.
- No Booked count, an ad that fits no pile, or the rules and the Friday review disagree. Nothing changes until [APPROVER NAME] decides.
- The starting budget, a raise or the month's spend would pass [MONTHLY AD BUDGET], or a new audience, area, offer, service or campaign is needed. The agent stops. The owner or [APPROVER NAME] decides.
- [APPROVER NAME] replies UNDO. The agent reverses that change and logs it.
- Payment, billing, spending limits, ad account ownership and admin rights stay with a person.
