## How the agent files use these

The agent files write blanks in [SQUARE BRACKETS]. Some use different words for the same fact:

- [BUSINESS NAME] and [COMPANY]: your company name.
- [TRADE]: your trade. [WEBSITE]: your website.
- [BUSINESS PHONE]: your main phone number.
- [SERVICE AREAS] and [SERVICE AREA]: the towns you serve. [AREAS YOU DO NOT SERVE]: the towns you don't.
- [PHONE HOURS]: the hours a person answers the phone. Outside them, the booking agent sends its after-hours text.
- [QUIET HOURS]: the hours when no text should go out.
- [BOOKING LINK]: your booking link. [DIRECT REVIEW LINK]: the link customers use to leave a review.
- [VISIT NAME] and [VISIT TYPE]: what you call the first visit.
- [REP NAME], [CALLER NAME] and [SALES OWNER]: your sales rep, the person who makes follow-up calls, and the person who owns open quotes. One person can be all three.
- [AVERAGE SOLD JOB]: what a typical job is worth.
- [OWNER NAME]: the owner's first name.
- [ALERT CHANNEL]: how alerts reach your team's phones.
- [SALES ALERT LIST]: who gets new-lead, follow-up and review alerts.
- [CREW ALERT LIST]: who on the crew gets filming alerts.
- [NUMBERS ALERT LIST]: who gets the alert when a number is trending down.
- [ALERT HOURS]: the hours alerts may go out.
- [MONTHLY AD BUDGET]: the most the ads agent may spend in a month.
- [APPROVER NAME]: who approves new wording and each new ad.
- The floors you wrote down: the data agent's line for each number it watches.
- [COST PER APPOINTMENT LINE]: the most you will pay for one booked appointment. The ads agent and the data agent both judge ads by it.
- [MONEY PER AD A DAY] and [DAYS BETWEEN RAISES]: the ads agent's daily spend per ad, and its wait between raises.
- [CLIPS FOLDER] and [EDITOR NAME]: where the crew's clips go, and who cuts videos by hand.
- [OUTCOME CHECK TIME]: when the sales rep is reminded to record a visit's outcome.
- [REVIEW TIME]: when the Friday review runs.

Each agent file also asks, under "What you need", for the facts only it needs.

Blanks inside a message, an alert or a name pattern ([NAME], [FIRST NAME], [PHONE], [ZIP], [JOB TYPE], [PROJECT], [DATE], [THEIR WORDS], [AMOUNT] and the like) are filled in each time from the contact's record or the ad. You never fill those.
