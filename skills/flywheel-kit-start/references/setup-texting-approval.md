# Get your texting approved (A2P) the first time
**Jobs-On-Demand Flywheel Kit · do this the same day you buy your GoHighLevel number**

> Phone carriers block automatic texts from a business until that business registers. The registration is called A2P 10DLC ("app to person" texts from a regular 10-digit local number). GoHighLevel handles it in its **Trust Center**. Until it's approved, your booking, follow-up and reviews agents can't send a single text. You fill in two forms: your **brand** (who your business is) and your **campaign** (what texts you send and how people agreed to get them). Filling them in takes about an hour. Review can take a few hours or a couple of weeks, so start today.

## What you need before you start
- **Your exact legal business name and EIN, as the IRS has them.** EIN is your federal tax ID. Copy them from your IRS letter (form CP 575 or 147C), not from memory.
- **Your business address**, matching the IRS records.
- **An email on your own website's domain** (like office@yourbusiness.com). A Gmail or Yahoo address can get the brand rejected.
- **A live website on your own domain** with more than one page: your services, your area, your phone and email. No "coming soon" or placeholder text.
- **A privacy policy page and a terms page** on that website, each with a texting section. Step 1 drafts both.
- **A public form on your website where people opt in.** "Opt in" means they tick a box agreeing to get texts. Step 1 drafts the words.
- **Your driver's license and your phone's camera.** GoHighLevel may ask you to verify your ID with a photo and a selfie. Check in your account.
- **No EIN?** If you have no EIN and no LLC or corporation, you can register as a **sole proprietor**. That path allows one number and about 3,000 texts a day. It texts a code to your cell phone, and you must reply within 24 hours. If you have an LLC, corporation or EIN, you can't use this path.
- **About $20 to $25** for the one-time fee, plus a small monthly fee. A rejection can add a review fee of about $15. Prices change, so check the fee page in your account.

## Step by step

1. `You + AI` **Fix your website first.** Reviewers visit your site before anything else. Ask your AI: "Draft templates A, B and C from SETUP-TEXTING-APPROVAL.md for [BUSINESS NAME]." Those are your privacy policy section, terms section and form consent boxes. Paste them onto your site.
   - Put the consent boxes on the form that brings leads in, and link the privacy policy and terms right next to them.
   - Link both pages in your website footer too. They must be real pages, not pop-ups.
   - Both boxes start unticked, and the form still sends if nobody ticks them.
   - The text boxes stay separate from any "I accept the terms" or email signup box.
   Done when: your privacy and terms pages load from your own domain, and the form shows the unticked boxes with both links beside them.

2. `You` **Register your brand.** In your sub-account, go to **Settings**, then **Phone System**, then **Trust Center**, and start the A2P registration. Answer "yes" to being in the US or Canada and to having a tax ID.
   - Type the legal name and EIN exactly as the IRS letter shows them, including "LLC" or "Inc".
   - Use the same address, your website, and your own-domain email for the contact person.
   - Pick **Low Volume Standard** (up to about 6,000 texts a day). That is plenty for one home service business.
   - A brand-new EIN can take a few weeks to show up in the records the reviewers check. If it's very new, expect a delay.
   Done when: your brand shows as submitted or approved in the Trust Center.

3. `You` **Start the campaign and pick the use case.** Open the **Campaigns** tab and create one for your brand. Choose **Manual Setup**, because your leads come from your own website form. Pick the **Mixed** use case. GoHighLevel then sets it to "Low Volume Mixed."
   - Why Mixed: your agents send booking replies, reminders and quote follow-ups, and review requests can read as marketing. Mixed covers both kinds.
   - Use the **Chat Widget Setup** path only if GoHighLevel's chat widget is the only place people give you their number.
   Done when: the campaign form is open with Mixed selected.

4. `You + AI` **Write the campaign description.** Say who you are, who gets your texts, what the texts are about, and how people opt in. Use template D. Ask your AI to fill it in with your trade and area.
   Done when: the description names your business and matches the texts your agents really send.

5. `You + AI` **Add your sample messages.** Paste the samples from template E that match what you'll send. The form asks for at least two.
   - Put your business name in every one, and end each with "Reply STOP to opt out."
   - Write fields like a name in [BRACKETS]. Don't paste GoHighLevel merge codes like {{contact.first_name}}.
   - If a sample has a link or a phone number, tick the matching "links" or "phone numbers" box. Never use bit.ly style short links.
   Done when: every sample sounds like a real text from your agents and the link and phone boxes match.

6. `You + AI` **Describe how people opt in.** Pick **website form** as the opt-in method. If you also run Facebook lead forms with a texting consent question, add that method too. Paste template F and the exact web address of the form page.
   - Reviewers open that address. It must load without a login and show the boxes.
   - If you list Facebook lead forms, add a link to a screenshot of that form's consent question.
   Done when: every way a person can agree to your texts is described, with a link a stranger can open.

7. `You + AI` **Set the opt-in, opt-out and help replies.** Paste template G into the opt-in message, and add the privacy and terms web addresses. Then check that STOP and HELP replies say your business name.
   - GoHighLevel may already answer STOP on its own. Check in your account which replies it sends and whether you can edit them.
   Done when: the opt-in, STOP and HELP replies each name your business, and HELP gives your phone or email.

8. `You` **Review and submit.** Click **Review Application**. GoHighLevel's checker flags problems before you can submit. Fix each one and click **Review Again**. Then submit and watch your email for the result.
   Done when: the campaign shows as Pending (under review). Don't submit a second copy while it's pending.

9. `You` **Link your number once it's approved.** Go to **Settings**, then **Phone System**, then **Phone Numbers**. Open the menu on your number and link it to the approved campaign.
   Done when: your number shows **A2P Verified**.

## Fill-in templates
**These are starting points, not finished legal text.** Edit every one to match the texts your agents really send. Swap each [BRACKET] for your details. If you're unsure about the law in your state, ask your lawyer.

**A. Privacy policy, texting section**
```
Text messaging. When you give [BUSINESS NAME] your mobile number and agree to texts, we use it only to send the texts you agreed to. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We may share information with companies that help us serve you, such as customer support. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Questions: [BUSINESS PHONE] or [BUSINESS EMAIL].
```

**B. Terms, texting section**
```
Text messages. [BUSINESS NAME] sends texts about your request, quotes, appointments, after-job check-ins and review requests. If you also agree to offers, we send occasional promotions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. We will send one text to confirm. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at [BUSINESS PHONE] or [BUSINESS EMAIL]. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Our Privacy Policy is at [WEBSITE]/privacy.
```

**C. Form consent boxes (both unticked, both optional)**
```
[ ] I agree to receive text messages from [BUSINESS NAME] about my request, quotes, appointments, after-job check-ins and review requests. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Agreeing is not required to buy anything.

[ ] I agree to receive occasional offers and promotions by text from [BUSINESS NAME]. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Agreeing is not required to buy anything.

Privacy Policy ([WEBSITE]/privacy) · Terms ([WEBSITE]/terms)
```

**D. Campaign description**
```
[BUSINESS NAME] is a [TRADE] company serving homeowners in [SERVICE AREA]. This campaign texts people who asked us for a quote or booked a visit on our website form at [WEBSITE]/[FORM PAGE]. Texts reply to new requests, book estimates and send appointment reminders. They also follow up on quotes we sent and check in after a job with a review request. Customers who tick our separate offers box get occasional promotions. We only text people who opted in.
```

**E. Sample messages (copy them from your agents)**
Reviewers compare your samples with the texts you really send. These are the kit's own messages, from the agent files, with your business name and the opt-out line added. Change them if you changed your agents' wording.
```
Booking reply (booking agent): Hey [FIRST NAME], it's [REP NAME] with [BUSINESS NAME]. Got your request about the [VISIT NAME]. I'm going to call you in the next couple minutes from [BUSINESS PHONE] so it's not a random number. If now's bad, just text me a better time. Reply STOP to opt out.

Visit confirmation (follow-up agent): Hi [FIRST NAME], it's [BUSINESS NAME]. You're booked for your [VISIT NAME] on [DATE] at [TIME]. Does that time still work? Reply STOP to opt out.

Quiet lead follow-up (follow-up agent): [BUSINESS NAME]: Still want me to take a look at [THE PROBLEM THEY DESCRIBED]? I've got [DAY] morning or [DAY] afternoon open. Reply STOP to opt out.

After-job check-in (reviews agent): Hi [FIRST NAME], it's [NAME] with [BUSINESS NAME]. I wanted to make sure yesterday's [PROJECT] was finished cleanly. Is everything looking and feeling right? Reply STOP to opt out.

Review ask (reviews agent): [BUSINESS NAME]: Glad everything landed the way it should. Would you mind sharing an honest review? [DIRECT REVIEW LINK] Reply STOP to opt out.

Offer (only if you send offers): [BUSINESS NAME]: [OFFER] for past customers through [DATE]. Reply BOOK to grab a spot. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

**F. How people opt in (message flow)**
```
Customers opt in on our website form at [WEBSITE]/[FORM PAGE]. They enter their name and phone number. They may tick an optional, unticked box to get texts from [BUSINESS NAME] about their request, quotes, appointments and after-job check-ins. A second optional box covers occasional offers. The boxes state message frequency, that message and data rates may apply, and to reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Links to our Privacy Policy ([WEBSITE]/privacy) and Terms ([WEBSITE]/terms) sit beside the boxes.
[Only if true:] Leads from our Facebook lead forms tick a matching optional texting consent question. Screenshot: [SCREENSHOT LINK].
```

**G. Opt-in, opt-out and help replies**
```
Opt-in confirmation: [BUSINESS NAME]: You're signed up for texts about your request and appointments. Message frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

STOP reply: [BUSINESS NAME]: You're unsubscribed and won't get any more texts from us. Reply START to sign up again.

HELP reply: [BUSINESS NAME]: For help, call [BUSINESS PHONE] or email [BUSINESS EMAIL]. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt-out words: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT
Help words: HELP, INFO
Opt-in words: START, UNSTOP (leave YES off: your agents ask yes-or-no questions, and a "yes" should not trigger a sign-up reply)
```

## Why approvals get rejected
In the Trust Center, open your rejected brand or campaign and click **View required fixes**. Fix every reason listed, not just the first, then resubmit.

| What you see | The fix |
|---|---|
| Brand rejected: name, EIN or address doesn't match | Copy them exactly from your IRS letter, including "LLC" or "Inc". A brand-new EIN may need a few weeks to show up. |
| Sole proprietor rejected, but you have an LLC or EIN | Register a new **Low Volume Standard** brand instead. Resubmitting the same one won't work. |
| Support email rejected | Use an email on your own website's domain, not Gmail or Yahoo. |
| Campaign description doesn't match the use case | Rewrite it so it matches Mixed and the samples (template D). |
| Sample messages unclear or missing your name | Use real messages, your business name, [BRACKETS] for fields, and "Reply STOP to opt out." |
| Opt-in error, or the call to action can't be verified | Give the exact form page address. It must load without a login and show the unticked text boxes. List every opt-in method. |
| Consent box pre-ticked, required, or joined to "I accept the terms" | Make separate, unticked, optional boxes for texts only (template C). |
| Privacy policy missing or can't be found | Put it on a real page on your domain, linked by the form and in the footer, with the no-sharing line (template A). |
| Terms missing texting details | Add business name, how often, rates, STOP, HELP, carrier line and privacy link (template B). |
| Website lacks business information | Add your services, area, phone and email, more than one page, and remove placeholder text. |
| Public link shortener | Use full web addresses in samples, never bit.ly style links. |
| Phone number or link setting doesn't match samples | Tick the "links" and "phone numbers" boxes to match what's in your samples. |

Not allowed at all: selling leads to other businesses, lending, debt collection, gambling, and alcohol, tobacco, vape or cannabis content. A normal home service business won't hit these.

## While you wait
- **Facebook and Instagram messages still work.** Your chat bot can keep answering them.
- **Calls and email still work.** Only texts wait.
- **Team alerts by app notification still work.** Alerts sent as texts wait.
- **Nothing else texts.** Keep the booking, follow-up and reviews agents' texting switched off until your number shows A2P Verified.
- **Hold off on ads that bring in text leads.** Leads that arrive now can't get a text back. Call them instead.
- **Don't work around it** by blasting texts from your personal phone. Carriers can flag and block your numbers.

Done when: your brand and campaign are approved and your number shows **A2P Verified**. You don't wait for this to keep going: once it's submitted, carry on with `SETUP-GOHIGHLEVEL.md` (in this folder) from step 6.
